♚ How to play

Place a bet on the Duel. You and the House are each dealt one piece from the hierarchy Pawn ‹ Knight ‹ Bishop ‹ Rook ‹ Queen ‹ King. The higher piece captures and pays even money.

On a tie, choose: Resign to surrender half your bet, or play the Gambit — match your bet, redeal, and you win on ties. Advancing into the Gambit is always the mathematically correct choice.

The optional King's Bounty side bet wins when your piece is a King. If both you and the House draw Kings, you win the Twin Kings progressive jackpot.

♚ Standoff — how to play

Place an Ante. You and the House each receive two pieces — yours face-up, the House's hidden. Looking at your hand, choose to Advance (match your Ante) or Resign (forfeit it).

The House then reveals and must qualify with a Rook-high hand or a pair. A pair beats two different pieces; otherwise the higher piece wins, ties broken by the second piece. On a tie the House keeps your Ante and returns your Advance.

Optimal strategy: Advance with any pair, a Queen-high hand or better, or Rook–Bishop. Otherwise Resign.

♚ Classic Duel — how to play

Place a bet on the Duel. One piece each; the higher (Pawn ‹ Knight ‹ Bishop ‹ Rook ‹ Queen ‹ King) captures and pays even money. On a tie, Resign for half or play the Gambit — match your bet, redeal, and you win on ties (always the correct play).

The King's Bounty side bet pays when your piece is a King; two Kings win the progressive jackpot.

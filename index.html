♚ How to play

Place an Ante. You and the House each receive two pieces — yours face-up, the House's hidden. Judge your hand and Advance (match your Ante) or Resign.

The House must qualify (Bishop-high or a pair). If it doesn't, both your bets push. A pair beats two singles; otherwise the higher piece wins, ties broken by the second.

Tip: Advance with a pair, a Queen-high hand, or Rook–Bishop.
